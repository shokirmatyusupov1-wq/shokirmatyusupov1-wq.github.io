Services

Care for every smile

Preventive care, treatment and cosmetic dentistry under one roof. Every treatment starts with a checkup and a plan you understand.

Checkups and X-rays

Every visit starts with listening. We check teeth, gums and bite, take low-dose digital X-rays when needed, and show you the images on screen.

Price: $69

Professional cleaning

Gentle removal of plaque and tartar, polishing and personal tips for brushing and flossing that fit your routine.

Price: $95

Tooth-colored fillings

Modern composite fillings matched to the color of your teeth, placed with local anesthetic so you stay comfortable.

Price: from $140

Dental implants

A titanium implant and a custom crown that look and work like a natural tooth. We plan each case with 3D scans.

Price: from $1,450

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening done by a dentist, with protection for your gums and clear advice on how to keep the result.

Price: $290

Children's dentistry

Short, calm visits for children, with simple explanations, sealants and fluoride to protect young teeth.

Price: from $45
