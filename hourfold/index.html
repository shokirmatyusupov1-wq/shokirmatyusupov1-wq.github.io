Features

Everything between "start timer" and "paid"

Built for freelancers who bill by the hour and would rather work than do paperwork.

  • One-click timer

    Start, pause and switch tasks from any device. Forgot to start? Add time by hand.

  • Clients and rates

    Set a rate per client or per project. Hourfold applies the right one automatically.

  • Invoices from tracked time

    Pick a date range and every tracked hour becomes a line on the invoice.

  • Any currency

    Bill clients abroad in USD, EUR or GBP, with your own tax settings.

  • Polite reminders

    Unpaid invoice? Hourfold sends a friendly reminder so you don't have to.

  • Clear reports

    See which clients and projects earn the most, week by week.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I add time I forgot to track?

Yes. You can add or edit entries by hand at any time, and they appear on the next invoice like any other hours.

Do my clients need an account?

No. Clients receive a link to view and download the invoice as a PDF.

Can I use different rates for different projects?

Yes. Set a default rate per client and override it for any project or single task.

Which currencies are supported?

In this concept, USD, EUR and GBP. Each client keeps its own currency.

Is Hourfold a real product?

No. Hourfold is a fictional product. This page was designed and built as a portfolio project to show landing page design and front-end development.
